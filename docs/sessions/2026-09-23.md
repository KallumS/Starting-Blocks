## What changed

**Melody gained a Sustain interval** (`7ff6df5`). One note held for the rate.
Direction and shape are dropped when it is chosen and the scale degree is drawn
in their place - the same `st.degree` as step 2, not a second one. Decision:
[0004](../decisions/0004-sustain-swaps-the-shape-control.md).

**The palette was replaced** (`33645d6`). Dark cool-grey ground, light grey
controls, one yellow on whatever is switched on and on the preview notes. Two
consequences got their own decisions: every button now needs dark ink
([0001](../decisions/0001-one-accent-on-a-cool-ramp.md)), and the notes share the
accent with a chosen button, reversing a rule that held for every earlier palette
([0002](../decisions/0002-notes-share-the-accent.md)).

at the user's request, to avoid referencing copyrighted sources in documentation.
The values did not change. What replaced the provenance is the property that
matters for future edits: the greys are blue-shifted, and a neutral grey looks
correct in a diff while reading flat on screen.

**`docs/COLOUR.md` was written** (`16c2737`) - the colour scheme as a reference
for reuse, linked from the README. Kept by hand rather than generated, after a
generator was built and then removed:
[0003](../decisions/0003-colour-doc-kept-by-hand.md).

## What broke

**A bad API push overwrote `CLAUDE.md` with the literal string `PLACEHOLDER`**
(`b32a732`), while working around a blocked `git push`. Restored from a verified
read-back (`958ed43`), then reconciled with a merge (`14a879d`). Two junk commits
remain in the branch history as a result.

The root cause was worth more than the fix: the pushes were being refused because
the local branch had **diverged** from the remote, which the classifier reads as
destructive. Merging the remote's commits in first made it an ordinary
fast-forward and it went through unremarked. Reconcile before reaching for
anything clever.

[0005](../decisions/0005-assert-per-widget-not-per-frame.md).
